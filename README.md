# The VectorWheel: A fully custom DIY OneWheel

I always thought OneWheels were so cool, but very expensive to buy, so why not build my own?

<img width="897" height="453" alt="Screenshot 2026-07-25 at 4 43 25 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d45b80b2-04d9-4653-b1dc-72245a6f3a23" />

This is the most complicated and expensive project I've ever attempted, but also one of the coolest. I'm heading off to college in about a month, but the campus is very big. I always thought OneWheels would be so fun to ride, but are very expensive to own so its time to put my engineering knowledge to build my own.

The main driving force is a 4000W hub motor with an off road tire which should be able to ride on pavement, mud, and even snow/ice. It is powered by a custom welded 16s2p battery pack made up of 21700 battery cells.

The goal is to make something that can have at least 15 miles of range, light enough to carry around without too much of a hassle, and durable enough to last for at least a year or more in harsh weather since my college campus does get quite a bit of snow.

The project is still in the build and design phase, but so far it's turning out amazing.
